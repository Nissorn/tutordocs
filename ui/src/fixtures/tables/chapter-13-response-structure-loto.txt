CANDIDATE RESPONSE STRUCTURE:
1. Immediate Hazard Assessment & Life Safety Prioritization
2. Invoking Stop Work Authority (No hesitation, no schedule bias)
3. Physical Barricade & Hazard Isolation
4. Formal Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Execution
5. Stakeholder Communication & Shift Handover
6. Root Cause and Corrective Action Plan
